We would like to inform you that during our recent inspection of the lift system at your premises, we have observed that the existing control panel is of an outdated configuration and is non-replaceable in parts.

In particular, the motherboard of the control panel is not repairable. In the event of any failure or malfunction of the motherboard, it will not be possible to carry out component-level repairs. Instead, the entire control system will have to be replaced.

Additionally, the following associated components, which are compatible only with the existing system, will also require complete replacement:

  • Car Operating Panel (COP) display card
  • Landing Operating Panel (LOP) display card
  • Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs)
  • Complete associated wiring

As these components are interdependent and not compatible with newer systems individually, partial replacement is not feasible.

We recommend considering a complete upgrade or replacement of the control system to ensure continued safe and reliable operation of the lift. Please feel free to contact us for any further clarification or to discuss the next course of action.

PWD MANDATORY ITEMS

As per PWD norms the followings are mandatory

1. Automatic Rescue Device (ARD): If for any reason, the ARD system is not in place at your society, Lift, then it needs to be installed / updated on priority basis, to ensure safe halting of the lift to nearest floor in case of power failures.

2. Toe Guard 750 mm: 750 mm standard requirement. If the existing one is less than 750 mm in height then it has to be urgently replaced. This prevents falling down of passengers while getting out of stuck elevators and also prevents toes getting stuck while getting inside the lift.

3. Car Top Safety Barricade - This will prevent falling of the person working / repairing standing on top of the elevator car.

4. Pit Switch (Pull-Push Button) - Up gradation will prevent accidental use of the button unknowingly

5. Overload Protection Device– If the passengers in the lifts are more than its capacity, this device will indicate to maintain the load capacity by voice command or Display Indicator.

6. Emergency Alarm – Emergency Alarm works like a siren in every emergency situation like power cut, etc.

7. Safety Grill for vision glass if door is swing door

8. Car Top Stop switch (Pull & push)
